    Money now

    Say how much you had on a day (usually the 1st of a month). Home then shows “All money”: what you had, plus income, minus expenses, minus money sent to people, plus money received from them. Moves between your accounts and jars change nothing. Hryvnias and euros are counted apart.

    The rate is typed by you and never looked up online. It gives only one line, “Together ≈ … ₴”. Amounts are never converted anywhere else.
